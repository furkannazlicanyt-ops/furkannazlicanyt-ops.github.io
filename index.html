Kanal Ajansi

Kanal Ajansi is a private tool that one channel owner uses to manage their own YouTube channels, Kept Receipts and Rudy Takes It Apart. It uploads and schedules the owner's own videos, and reads those channels' statistics and comments. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app uses YouTube API Services to access the owner's channels: channel details, videos, playlists, captions, comments and YouTube Analytics reports.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and schedule the owner's videos and to review how their channels perform. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and does not collect data from channel viewers beyond what YouTube returns for the owner's own channels, such as public comments.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Stored data can be removed by deleting it from the owner's computer.
